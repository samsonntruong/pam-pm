Step 1: Model

Set META_MODEL, META_BASE_URL, and META_API_KEY in your secrets file, then test.

Step 2: Slack

The team lead or a workspace admin creates ONE internal Slack app once from the manifest in slack-app-manifest.yaml. Everyone else installs nothing and just gets DMs from Pam. If app installs are restricted in the workspace, ask a workspace admin to approve the install.

Set SLACK_BOT_TOKEN in your secrets file, then test.
