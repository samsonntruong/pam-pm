Step 1: Model

Add META_MODEL, META_BASE_URL, and META_API_KEY to your secrets file, then check the connection.

Step 2: Slack

The team lead or a workspace admin creates ONE internal Slack app once from the file slack-app-manifest.yaml. Everyone else installs nothing and just gets messages from Pam. If installs need approval in your workspace, ask a workspace admin to approve it.

Add SLACK_BOT_TOKEN to your secrets file, then check the connection.

Tell your team

Copy and send this to the team:

Pam was added to the workspace. You will get a morning message about your work. Your answers stay private. There is nothing to install.

Step 3: Azure DevOps

Only needed when Azure DevOps is chosen above. Add ADO_ORG_URL, ADO_PROJECT, and ADO_PAT to your secrets file, then check the connection.

Step 4: People

Add teammates who will get the morning message, one name per line. Names save with this step.

Step 5: Rules
